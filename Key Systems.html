06 / SYSTEMIZE · AUTOMATE · DELEGATE

Leverage, not labor

Stop bleeding time
on what doesn't matter
01

Systemize

Document it. If it's in your head, it doesn't exist. Write it down, map it out, make it repeatable.

02

Automate

Use technology. If a machine can do it, a machine should do it. Remove yourself from the loop.

03

Delegate

Hand it to a human — but not just any handoff. Delegate = Ownership + Outcome.

D.R.I.Directly Responsible Individual

Every task, every project, every outcome has one person who owns it. Not a committee. Not "the team." One name. One owner.

STEP 01

Pain

Identify your pain points. Where are you stuck? What drains you?

STEP 02

Audit

Audit your energy and time. Where is it actually going?

STEP 03

Fill

Fill your calendar with high-value activities only.

STEP 04

Transfer

Clear delegation. Hand off everything that isn't your highest and best use.

08 / THE 3 BELIEF BLOCKERS

The lies entrepreneurs tell themselves

Name them.
Then move past them.
✕
LIE 01

"I'm special"

You think your business is beyond comparison. Reality: most of what you do mirrors common business loops. Systems already exist for much of it.

✕
LIE 02

"They'll mess it up"

Fear of delegating keeps you doing everything. Maybe true now — but without training you stay stuck. Holding on costs more than letting go.

✕
LIE 03

"I can't build a system"

You built the business — you can document the process. Stop lying to yourself.
